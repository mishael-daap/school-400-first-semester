## Seven Stages of Action

- Divided into execution and evaluation.
- **Execution:** intention, specify action, execute.
- **Evaluation:** perceive, interpret, evaluate.
- The model explains how users move from a goal to an action to judging the result.

## Gulf of Execution and Gulf of Evaluation

- **Gulf of execution:** the gap between what the user wants to do and knowing how to do it.
- **Gulf of evaluation:** the gap between what the system did and the user understanding it.

## Model Human Processor

- Screen — perceptual — cognitive — motor — button.
- Perceptual to sensory memory, sensory memory to working memory, and working memory to long term memory.
- **Perceptual processor:** receives information through the senses — sight, sound and touch.
- **Cognitive processor:** interprets information, remembers, reasons and makes decisions. Example: deciding which account to withdraw from.
- **Motor processor:** carries out physical actions — clicking, typing, tapping, etc.

## Human Memory Systems

There are three of them:

- **Sensory memory:** briefly holds the information we see and hear.
- **Working memory:** holds what we are thinking about.
- **Long term memory:** stores knowledge and experience.

## Chunking

- Grouping information together so that it is easier to process.

## How Humans Process Information

- Receive — process — respond.

## Mental Models

- A mental model is the understanding a user builds about how a system works and what will happen when they act.
- They are formed from past experience, similar apps, instructions and labels.
