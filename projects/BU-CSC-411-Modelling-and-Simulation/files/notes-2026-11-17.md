## Model

A model is a pattern, plan, representation or description designed to show the main object or workings of an object, system or concept.

## Practical Situations

- **Industrial:** queuing systems, networks, inventory, chemical process.
- **Business and economic:** consumer behaviour.
- **Social:** population dynamics, environmental health.
- **Biological:** fluid balance, electrolytes, blood cells, brain activity.
- **Military:** war strategies and tactics.
- **Campus:** hostel allocation, shuttle routes, exam timetable.

## Example of a Model

Walking from the hostel to this class.

```
s = v × t
```

- `s` — the distance.
- `v` — the walking speed.
- `t` — the time observed.

## Characteristics of a Model

- **Over-simplified:** ignoring important parameters is a problem. Over-simplification.
- **A useful model:** ignoring unimportant detail is not a problem, it is the point.
- **The real system:** a model is never equal to the real system, it is always simpler.
